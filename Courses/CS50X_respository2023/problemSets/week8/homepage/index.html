Minecraft is a sandbox game, which focuses on:
  • Gathering resources
  • Building structures
  • Working with redstone
  • Multiplayer

Within Surival, players must gather all resources needed to build and can die. In Creative, players are unable to die (unless they change to Survival) and can select any and all resources they want to use. Minecraft also allows players to play multiplayer, whether it’s survival or a Vs. game in creative.
